## Refunds, returns, and order policy (Acme Storefront)

This is the operating policy our team works from. It supersedes anything
the storefront UI says by default — if a customer asks for a refund or
exchange, follow this document first and only escalate to the founder
when the case is outside these rules.

### 1. Refund eligibility

We accept refund requests within 30 calendar days of the original
delivery date as recorded by the carrier. Customers must provide either
the order ID or the email used at checkout. After 30 days, refunds are
denied unless the item is defective on arrival ("DOA") and the customer
flagged the defect within 7 days of delivery.

Defective items are refunded at full purchase price including original
shipping. Buyer's-remorse returns are refunded at purchase price minus
the original shipping fee; return shipping is the customer's
responsibility.

### 2. Restocking fee

For non-defective returns of opened items, we charge a 15% restocking
fee on the item subtotal (not on shipping). Sealed/unused items are
exempt. The restocking fee is disclosed on the return-confirmation
email before the return ships, never silently deducted at refund time.

### 3. Refund processing time

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3 business
days of receiving the returned item at our warehouse. Bank settlement
is typically an additional 3–5 business days; we do not control that
window and we tell customers so up front.

### 4. Exchanges

Same-SKU exchanges (size or color swap) are free within the 30-day
window — we cover return shipping AND replacement shipping. Different-
SKU exchanges are processed as a refund-and-rebuy and the customer pays
shipping on the new order.

### 5. Items not eligible for refund

- Custom or personalized items (engraving, monogram)
- Final-sale clearance (clearly marked at checkout)
- Digital downloads after first successful download
- Gift cards

For these categories the only remediation is store credit on a
case-by-case basis at the founder's discretion.

### 6. Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund up until the carrier has
scanned the package. Once scanned, cancellations are processed as
returns under section 1.

### 7. Damaged-in-transit claims

Photo evidence of the damaged package and contents is required within
48 hours of delivery. Claims older than 48 hours are denied because the
carrier's own claim window has typically closed by then.
